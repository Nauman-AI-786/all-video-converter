FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on how many files you convert.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens on your device. Only the converter engine itself is downloaded the first time you convert something.

Can I convert a full movie?

Usually not here. Movies are large, and a browser has limited memory and speed. Use a desktop converter such as HandBrake for movies, and use Vidmorph for short videos, music and GIFs.

Why is it slower than desktop software?

Browsers run FFmpeg slower than a native app. Big or HD files can take a few minutes, and phones are slower than computers. Keep the tab open while converting.

Why did my file fail?

The file may be damaged, DRM-protected, too big for your browser's memory, or in a rare codec. Sound-only files can only become audio formats. Try a smaller file or a lower size/quality setting.

Which formats work?

Most video and audio files go in. Out: MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, 3GP, FLV, WMV, MPEG, OGV, TS, GIF, MP3, AAC, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA.
